What this is

Two Epic Cash wallets build a payment together. When they are not online at the same time, an Epicbox relay holds the payment, encrypted, until the receiving wallet collects it. This server is one of those relays.

It runs epicmail, an Epicbox relay written in Rust. The relay stores and passes on encrypted messages only. It never holds funds.
